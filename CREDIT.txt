    Version used:   see gradle.properties / build metadata of that project

  WHAT WAS ADAPTED
    The spaceship physics and rocket propulsion design adopted by Northstar
    Redux is derived from Create: Cosmonautics, specifically:

      - Rigid-body spaceship movement replacing purely kinematic, vertically
        constrained contraption movement.
      - Simulated thrust modelling: per-thruster force application, continuous
        fuel/propellant consumption during burn, and thrust-vector resolution
        from thruster placement and vessel orientation.
      - Mass and centre-of-mass accounting for assembled vessels.
      - Sublevel ("ship") assembly, pose and transform handling.
      - The corresponding Ponder scenes, tooltip wording and guidebook text
        describing the above.

  The corresponding source files of Create: Cosmonautics are distributed under
  GPL-3.0. As required by GPL-3.0 sections 4 and 5, the complete corresponding
  source of this project is available under the same license, and every
  retained copyright notice from Create: Cosmonautics must be preserved in the
  individual files to which it applies.

  ASSETS
    No textures, models, sounds, music or other binary media were copied from
